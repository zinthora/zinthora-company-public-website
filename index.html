Technology That Cares for You

We believe digital products should make the world a little calmer, kinder and healthier. These are the principles we build on.

Calm by Design

No chaos and nothing shouting for your attention. Our products are tuned for stillness rather than stimulation, so opening them feels like a moment of peace.

Made to Be Put Down

We do not measure success in screen time. Our products know when to stop, and gently invite you to rest instead of offering one more thing to watch.

Safe Before It Reaches You

Nothing is published first and moderated later. What you see has already been verified, so your feed is never the place where a problem is discovered.

Kind Spaces

We leave out the mechanics people use to be unkind to each other. No comment wars, no lists of names to read, compare or worry about.

Worth Over Popularity

No like counts and no view counts. You decide what is worth your time by what is in front of you, not by what a number says other people decided.

Respect for Your Attention

No ads, no tracking across apps and no urgent notifications. Your attention belongs to you, and we build as though that is true.

Harmony

Only what’s worth watching finds its way to you

A quiet place for short videos. Open it and you get one video at a time: a sunset somebody filmed on their way home, a line worth remembering set over a picture, a quiet piece of music. Other feeds are asked to show you something interesting — Harmony is asked to show you something worth watching.

  • Every video is verified before it reaches the feed
  • No like counts, no view counts, no comments and no direct messages
  • The feed knows when to stop: a quiet rest page after a long stretch of watching
  • Nobody is told who liked a video, only that somebody did
  • Few and gentle notifications, none of them urgent
  • Unhurried posting with video, photos, text, music and voice-over
